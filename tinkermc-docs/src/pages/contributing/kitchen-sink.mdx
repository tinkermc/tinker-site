## Inline Formatting, Badges & Footnotes

Status badges: :badge[Stable]{success}, :badge[Beta]{warning}, :badge[Experimental]{danger}, and :badge[Info]{info}.

Standard formatting supports *italics*, **bold**, ***bold italics***, ~~strikethrough~~, inline `code`, and footnotes[^1][^note].

> Quoted text can span multiple lines.
> You can also use **Markdown** and `inline code` inside a blockquote.

[^1]: Standard numbered footnote rendered at the bottom of the page.
[^note]: Named footnotes render with automatic numbering and anchor links.
